:page-title: The Wheel
:page-description: About riding the London Eye

The Wheel of Fortune can be seen from afar
 +
Its presence is simply awe-inspiring
 +
Entering the pod of life, senses heighten
 +
As all around can be freely observed

The journey of the pod is measured
 +
By the altering inclination of the surrounds
 +
Striving ever higher and higher into the sky
 +
To reach the pinnacle of the journey

Whereupon time moves perceptibly slower,
 +
As if one were approaching the speed of light
 +
Viewing the world from up on high
 +
Frees us from the cares and troubles of life

All that remains is a sense of awe
 +
At seeing a wonderful city in a whole new light
 +
Wishing those you care about could be with you,
 +
To experience the moment when time stands still

The moment in time has passed
 +
Slowly crashing back down to earth
 +
Wishing to be in that moment forever
 +
Longing to experience the pleasure once more

That is the joy of the London Eye
 +
Allowing the traveller
 +
For a brief moment
 +
To be an Eye on London
