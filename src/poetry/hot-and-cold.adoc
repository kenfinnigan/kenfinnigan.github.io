== Hot & Cold
:page-title: Hot and Cold
:page-description: A bit of a silly poem

From the Earth a man rose,
 +
With simple thoughts and attire.
 +
Though he no doubt feared the crows,
 +
He had a need which was more dire,
 +
Keeping warm by a fire.

As he searched for wood and bark,
 +
Wood was found, and a strange can.
 +
He drank the can for a lark,
 +
And though he felt like eating ham,
 +
All he wanted was a fan.
